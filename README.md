## 🔨 1. Building & Islands

- [x] **Block Placement & Building:** Place and break blocks on your private floating island with smooth grid alignment. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Smart Stairs:** Stair blocks automatically turn into corners and connect smoothly when placed together. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **40k Block Limit:** Generous 40,000 block building limit per island so you can build massive creations without running out of space. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Lag-Free Block Optimization:** Adjacent blocks automatically merge together behind the scenes, keeping the game smooth even with huge islands. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Backpack & Hotbar:** Clean inventory menu with item categories and a quick hotbar to switch tools easily. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Airship Skins & Banners:** Customize your island with decorative flags, lanterns, and paint colors for your airship. `[PLANNED]`

## 🌾 2. Farming & Nature

- [x] **Crops, Trees & Bushes:** Grow multiple crops (wheat, tomato, carrot, potato), trees (birch, pine, apple, lemon), and bushes (blueberry and raspberry). `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Watering Cans & Sickles:** Till dirt with a hoe, speed up crop growth with watering cans, and harvest faster using tiered sickles. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Day & Night Cycle:** Dynamic daylight, glowing twilight sunsets, and starry nights with soft wind effects across the sky. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Farm Animals:** Raise chickens for eggs, sheep for wool, and cows for milk in fenced pastures on your island. `[PLANNED]`

---

## ⛏️ 3. Mining & Tools

- [x] **Ore Nodes & Pickaxes:** Mine stone, coal, iron, and gold nodes that spawn around your island using tiered pickaxes. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Player Skills & Leveling:** Level up your Farming, Mining, and Woodcutting skills to unlock better tools, faster speeds, and rare seeds. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Tool Animations:** Custom swing, till, chop, and mining animations with smooth spring physics for all tools. `[IN PROGRESS]`

## 📦 4. Storage & Workstations

- [x] **Multiple Chests:** Multiple chest types to store your items, including normal storage chests and automated chests for factory systems. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Crafting Workstations:** Multiple crafting workstations to create tools, seeds, decorative blocks, and building parts. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Droppable Items (Q / F):** Press Q to drop items on the ground and press F while aiming to pick them up. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Safe Inventory System:** Items move smoothly between inventory, chests, and crafting without risk of disappearing or duplicating. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Furnaces & Composters:** Smelt raw iron and gold into ingots, or turn organic scraps into fertilizer to speed up crops. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Industrial Equipment:** Heavy-duty processing machines including advanced smelters, stonecutters, and high-throughput fabricators. `[IN PROGRESS]`

## 🧑‍🌾 5. Villagers & Helpers

- [x] **Villager Helpers:** Place a bed to invite villagers to live on your island. Farmers till and harvest; miners break ores for you. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Villager Dwarfs:** Mini villager helpers that handle single dedicated tasks for your villagers, like harvesting crops or hauling items. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Smart Villager Walking:** Villagers can walk up stairs, navigate around walls, and easily find their way around your builds. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Warrior Villager Class:** Recruit combat-ready warrior villagers to guard your island and patrol the grounds. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Gardener Villager Class:** Specialized gardener NPCs that care for flower beds, prune fruit trees, and tend berry bushes. `[IN PROGRESS]`

---

## ⚙️ 6. Factory Automation

- [x] **Factory Transport Pipes:** Connect machines, chests, and crops with pipes to move items around automatically. You can see items traveling inside. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Funnel Pipes:** Special funnel pipes that pick up dropped items from the ground and convert them into pipe transport items automatically. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Sprinklers & Auto-Harvesters:** Build sprinkler systems to water whole fields at once, along with conveyor belts and sorters. `[PLANNED]`

## 🧭 7. Biomes & Exploration

- [x] **Personal Island Airship:** Every island comes with a dock and an airship ready to take you into the skies. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Biome 1: Golem Paradise:** A mysterious sky biome filled with ancient stone golems, ruins, and rare resources to explore. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Biome 2: The Wetlands:** A lush, misty swamp island biome featuring waterlogged flora, rich clay deposits, and humid weather. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Visiting Other Islands:** Browse online players and visit their islands. Owners can set permissions, block players, or make their island private. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Island Previews:** See a snapshot of an island and its owner's level before you fly there. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Sky Dungeons & Monsters:** Fly to mysterious storm islands with your friends to fight monsters, solve puzzles, and win rare loot. `[PLANNED]`

## 🪙 8. Economy & Trading

- [ ] **Vending Machines:** Placeable vending machine shops that allow visitors to buy and sell items on your island automatically. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Coins & Currency:** In-game economy with gold coins earned from selling crops, fulfilling island orders, and trading. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Public Market Hub:** A central sky town where players gather to meet wandering merchants and buy rare seeds and blueprints. `[PLANNED]`

## ⚡ 9. Performance & Onboarding

- [x] **Full Mobile Support:** Full touch controls and mobile UI for phones and tablets, including quick hotbar switching and tap-to-harvest prompts. `[COMPLETE]`
- [x] **Streaming Enabled:** Smart object streaming so dense islands, massive builds, and machines load smoothly without crashing or lagging. `[COMPLETE]`
- [ ] **Player Onboarding:** Interactive beginner tutorial, quest guidance, and hints to help new players get started on their island. `[IN PROGRESS]`
- [ ] **Server Load Testing:** Stress-testing server performance to find the optimal player count per server while keeping physics, villagers, and pipes at high FPS. `[IN PROGRESS]`
