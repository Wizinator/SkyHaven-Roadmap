Block Placement & Building

Adjacent blocks automatically merge together behind the scenes, keeping the game smooth even with huge islands.

Backpack & Hotbar

Clean inventory menu with item categories and a quick hotbar to switch tools easily.

Farming & Nature

Agriculture Complete

Crops, Trees & Bushes

Grow multiple crops (wheat, tomato, carrot, potato), trees (birch, pine, apple, lemon), and bushes (blueberry and raspberry).

Tools Complete

Watering Cans & Sickles

Till dirt with a hoe, speed up crop growth with watering cans, and harvest faster using tiered sickles.

World Complete

Day & Night Cycle

Dynamic daylight, glowing twilight sunsets, and starry nights with soft wind effects across the sky.

Animals Planned

Farm Animals

Raise chickens for eggs, sheep for wool, and cows for milk in fenced pastures on your island.

Mining & Tools

Mining Complete

Ore Nodes & Pickaxes

Skills Complete

Player Skills & Leveling

Level up your Farming, Mining, and Woodcutting skills to unlock better tools, faster speeds, and rare seeds.

Animation In Progress

Tool Animations

Custom swing, till, chop, and mining animations with smooth spring physics for all tools.

Storage & Workstations

Storage Complete

Multiple Chests

Multiple chest types to store your items, including normal storage chests and automated chests for factory systems.

Crafting Complete

Crafting Workstations

Multiple crafting workstations to create tools, seeds, decorative blocks, and building parts.

Gameplay Complete

Droppable Items (Q / F)

Inventory Complete

Safe Inventory System

Furnaces & Composters
